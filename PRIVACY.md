# Privacy Policy

Zen mode for X does not collect personal information. It has no server and no analytics. All preferences are stored on your device in the browser's local extension storage.

Influence warnings are off by default. When you enable them and save your own TypeSafe API key, the extension sends visible public post text, quoted text, and link paths directly from your browser to TypeSafe's API to obtain an estimate. Private messages, drafts, images, videos, cookies, and profile histories are not sent. The key stays in the extension's private storage on your device and is never included in settings exports. Results are cached on your device for 30 days.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at https://github.com/quasa0/zen-mode-for-x/issues.
